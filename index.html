history/making of dragon ball z battle of gods

dragon ball z battle of gods was made in 2013 and was made by then akira toriyama and it would have been 16 years since dragonball ended in 1997.
nothing new came out in terms of a new dragonball anime but dragon ball was still one of the most popular shows in the world and it was still being watched by millions of people around the world.
however there was a movie made in april 2009 called dragon ball evolution and it was the worst dragonball movies and one of the worst movies in general ever.
it was a live action movie instead of an animated one and they completely ruined the story and characters and it was a huge disappointment to fans of the series.
for example goku was a teenager instead of a child and he was also a high school student instead of a martial artist and he was arrogant and rude instead of kind and humble.
due to how bad they treated akira toriyama during the production of the movie and how bad the movie was akira toriyama decided to make a new dragonball movie and thus made dragon ball z battle of gods.
this movie was the first dragonball movie to be made in 17 years and it was a huge success and it was the first dragonball movie to be made in 3d.
because of how popular the movie was it was decided to make a new dragonball anime series called dragon ball super and it was also made by akira toriyama and revived the dragonball franchise.
